Local Assistant setup

Keep your Mac online and signed into the same Messages account as your iPhone.

  1. In Terminal, open the downloaded source folder and run bash scripts/start.sh. Follow the prompts to choose a model and connect your self-chat.
  2. Stop the Terminal assistant, then run bash scripts/install-mac-app.sh in the same folder.
  3. In the menu bar app, choose Access settings and enable Full Disk Access for Local Assistant. Quit and reopen the app after changing access.
  4. Choose Connect under Source access and approve the permissions you want to grant.
  5. Start the assistant. Send /status, then ask “What's on my calendar tomorrow?”

Daily use

Send /resume to enable reminders or /pause to pause them. Turn on Start at login in the menu bar app to start it when you sign in.

If a source is unavailable, check its permission in System Settings. Under Recent activity, use Open log folder to see startup errors.
